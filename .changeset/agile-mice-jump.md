**Preflight guarded install destinations before replacing gsd-core/** — the installer checks the gsd-core root, guarded layout artifact destinations, and selected Codex config and agent TOML destinations before installation writes. Codex installs also preflight hooks.json when projected hook reconciliation is enabled and the scoped migration plan leaves hooks.json and config.toml unchanged; unrelated pending migrations and absence of install state alone do not bypass this check. Planned mutations of those inputs remain an exception: in a migration 002 upgrade containing legacy GSD hooks, the migration removes or replaces the hooks.json symlink without the opt-in, and the reconciler checks the resulting destination after the gsd-core refresh. Refusals caught by preflight preserve the previous install; nested corpus symlinks retain their existing replacement behavior. Artifact plan builders, writers, and applySurface share the config-dir fallback for an empty home; the committed surface resolves before the gsd-core refresh, and orphan recovery runs after destination preflight.
